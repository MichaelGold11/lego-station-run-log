Optional: stop the certificate warning

Tapping through the warning is fine. To stop it, trust this laptop's certificate once. It can only vouch for this laptop's .local name and private network addresses, never for any other website. It only helps with the link that has .local in it.

  1. Download this laptop's certificate and tap Allow.
  2. Settings > General > VPN & Device Management: tap the profile, then Install.
  3. Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings: turn it on.
